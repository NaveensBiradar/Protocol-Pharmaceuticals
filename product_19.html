Product introduction
Feronia -XT Drop is a pediatric iron and folic acid supplement formulated to help support healthy iron levels and red blood cell development in infants. It contains a combination of ferrous ascorbate (iron) and folic acid (vitamin B9), which are essential for various physiological processes during early growth. Iron is a vital mineral necessary for the formation of hemoglobin, which ensures the proper transport of oxygen from the lungs to all parts of the body, maintaining energy and focus. Folic acid plays an essential role in rapid cell division and healthy red blood cell production, helping to manage nutritional deficiencies in children.

    How FeroZoom works
    FeroZoom Drops contains elemental iron, folic acid, which work to support healthy blood production and nerve function. Elemental iron is a critical building block for haemoglobin, the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen throughout your body. Folic acid is a B12-vitamin necessary for making new cells, including red blood cells, and is vital for preventing certain developmental complications. Cyanocobalamin (vitamin B12) helps maintain healthy nerve cells and works alongside folic acid to ensure proper red blood cell development and DNA synthesis. Together, these ingredients help address the underlying causes of nutritional anaemia.

    How Achievit works
    Achievit Drops contains elemental iron, folic acid, which work to support healthy blood production and nerve function. Elemental iron is a critical building block for haemoglobin, the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen throughout your body. Folic acid is a B12-vitamin necessary for making new cells, including red blood cells, and is vital for preventing certain developmental complications. Cyanocobalamin (vitamin B12) helps maintain healthy nerve cells and works alongside folic acid to ensure proper red blood cell development and DNA synthesis. Together, these ingredients help address the underlying causes of nutritional anaemia.

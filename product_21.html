Product introduction
AeroFresh Kid is an anti-allergic medicine for children that contains levocetirizine and montelukast. It is used to relieve allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, nasal congestion, and watery eyes. It works by blocking substances that trigger allergic reactions in the body. Common side effects may include headache, stomach pain, dry mouth, and tiredness. This medicine should be used with caution in children with kidney problems. AeroFresh Kid is commonly prescribed in children to treat symptoms of allergies, such as a runny nose, sneezing, itching, swelling, congestion, and watery eyes. It may also help in the treatment of asthma and skin allergies. Give AeroFresh Kid to your child exactly as prescribed by the doctor, in the recommended dose and duration. It can be given with or without food. Try to give it at the same time each day to help maintain a consistent effect. If your child vomits within 30 minutes of taking this medicine, give the same dose again.
Benefits of AeroFresh Kid
Treatment of Allergic skin conditions Allergic skin conditions can cause itching, redness, and rashes due to an overactive immune response. AeroFresh Kid helps reduce the action of chemicals responsible for allergic reactions. This relieves itching, redness, and swelling, helping soothe the skin and improve comfort. It helps control symptoms and prevent flare-ups when used regularly.
Treatment of Allergic rhinitis Allergic rhinitis is triggered by a reaction to allergens, such as pollen, resulting in symptoms including sneezing, a runny nose, congestion, and itchy eyes. AeroFresh Kid works by blocking allergy-causing chemicals and reducing inflammation in the airways. This helps relieve nasal and eye symptoms and makes breathing easier. Give it to your child for as long as it is prescribed to help maintain symptom control and prevent recurrence.

    How AeroFresh Kid works
    AeroFresh Kid is a combination of two medicines: levocetirizine and montelukast. Levocetirizine is an antiallergic medicine that blocks a chemical messenger called histamine, which causes symptoms such as a runny nose, watery eyes, and sneezing. Montelukast is a leukotriene antagonist that blocks another chemical messenger called leukotriene. This helps reduce inflammation (swelling) in the airways and nose and improves allergy symptoms.
